Tyre shops in Singapore quietly mark up the brand more than the rubber.

Standard-spec fair prices:
• One tyre, fitted: S$130–300
• Set of four: S$520–1,200
• 4-wheel alignment: S$60–120
• Balancing (x4): S$40–80

These are guides. The actual number moves with the tyre's size, width and brand. A mid-range tyre handles SG roads as well as the premium one for half the cost.

A good shop will show you the worn pads, the before-and-after alignment readout, and explain why each line is there.
