Most JB breakdowns are avoidable. They start at home, before you ever pull out of the driveway.

Eight checks before you join the Causeway queue:
• Tyres + spare, all with tread
• Brakes that bite without a shudder
• Coolant topped up, no weeping
• Battery with life left (test it if it's over 3 years old)
• Every light working, both ends
• Wipers that clear properly
• Three-quarter tank to leave SG (it's the law)
• VEP, working IU, insurance extended to Malaysia

The stuff that kills road trips: dead battery in the Causeway jam, a tyre that gives up on the NSE, a flat phone with no GPS, an insurer who didn't know you crossed the border.

Run through it the morning before you go. It takes five minutes.
