Any workshop quote that just says "general servicing" is hiding things. You're allowed to ask what.

Five things to do with every quote you get:
• Read every line. Padding hides in the line items, so read each one.
• Check each part is named and which brand.
• Question anything vague or bundled.
• Ask why the labour costs what it does. Most SG workshops charge by job, so the maths should make sense.
• Get it in writing. Take it home. Sit on it.

The good workshops welcome the questions. The defensive ones tell you everything you need to know.
