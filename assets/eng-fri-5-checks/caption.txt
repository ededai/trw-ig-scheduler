Before you pay any workshop in Singapore a diagnostic fee, there are five quick things worth checking yourself first.

Start the car and note which warning lights stay on, then pull the dipstick and look at the oil colour, because golden is fine but jet black means you're overdue. While you're under there, check how old the tyres and battery are. Listen for any new noise, and sniff for anything burning or sweet. None of it costs a cent, and it usually tells you whether the problem's urgent or can sit till the weekend.
