A clean service book is not a clean car.

Most used cars get sold on the pitch. The condition usually tells a different story. The bay's been waxed shiny, the paint's been freshened up, the seller swears it was driven only on Sundays.

The condition tells the truth: original paint over respray, an honest engine bay (the kind that hasn't been wiped down for show), tyres with real age and tread, dashboard lights that cycle and clear on start-up.

Save this before your next viewing. Or send it to the friend who "found a great deal".
