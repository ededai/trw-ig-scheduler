That brand-new tyre your shop just fitted in Singapore might've been built before your last car.

The four digits inside the oval on the sidewall tell you the real age: first two are the week, last two are the year. The one in this post reads 1001, so week 10 of 2001, which makes that tyre older than some drivers on the road. Rubber hardens on the shelf long before it ever touches tarmac, and our heat only speeds it up.

Check yours, then DM us the four digits if you want a straight answer on whether it's still safe. Save this for your next tyre change.
