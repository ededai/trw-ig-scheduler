LTA today proposed a new COE system for cars. Cat A and Cat B would merge into one category, and buyers would then get money off or pay extra depending on how expensive their car is. It is only a proposal for now, and LTA wants public feedback by 2 November 2026.

What LTA proposed today

Small cars and big cars would bid together and pay the same COE price. After that, the price is adjusted by the value of the car.

Your carWhat happens to your COE price
Cheaper modelRebate of up to $15,000
Mid-range modelNo change
Pricier modelSurcharge of up to $15,000

How the rebate and surcharge would work

LTA would rank every car model by its value (its open market value, or OMV) and sort them into bands. Your band decides what you pay on top of, or off, the COE price. LTA is choosing between two options:

OptionBands
Three bands$15,000 off · no change · $15,000 extra
Five bands$15,000 off · $7,500 off · no change · $7,500 extra · $15,000 extra
LTA's own example: the Toyota Sienta. It would get $15,000 off under three bands, or $7,500 off under five. The bands would be updated every year, so a model can move between them.

Why LTA wants to change the COE

Cat A was meant to keep COEs cheaper for smaller, mass-market cars. That has stopped working. At yesterday's bidding on 7 October, Cat A closed at $130,001 and Cat B at $130,100.

Only $99 separated a small-car COE from a big-car COE.

Linking the COE to the car's price is LTA's way of making pricier cars pay more again.

What is not decided yet

  • Three bands or five.
  • Whether Cat E (Open) is removed or kept for cars only.
  • Whether the rebate and surcharge also apply when you renew your COE.
  • When any of this starts. LTA will share its decision in the first half of 2027.

What the new COE proposal means for you

Buying a car soon: nothing changes yet. COE bidding runs on today's Cat A and Cat B rules until LTA says otherwise. See the latest prices on our COE results page.

Renewing your COE soon: today's rules apply. LTA has said it could consider transitional arrangements for current owners. Our COE renewal vs scrap guide helps you decide.
