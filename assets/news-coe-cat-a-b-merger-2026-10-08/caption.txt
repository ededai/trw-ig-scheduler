LTA wants one COE for all cars. Here is what that means, in plain words.

Today, small cars bid in Cat A and bigger cars bid in Cat B. Under LTA's proposal, everyone bids in one queue and pays the same COE price.

Then the price gets adjusted by how expensive your car model is:
Cheaper models get up to $15,000 off.
Mid-range models pay the COE price as is.
Pricier models pay up to $15,000 more.

The reason: on 7 October, a Cat A COE cost $130,001 and a Cat B COE cost $130,100. A $99 gap. In June 2025 that gap was $16,001.

Still undecided: three bands or five, what happens to Cat E, and whether COE renewals get the rebate or surcharge too.

Nothing changes yet. Feedback closes 2 November 2026, 11.59pm at go.gov.sg/coe-pcd-feedback. LTA shares its decision in the first half of 2027.
