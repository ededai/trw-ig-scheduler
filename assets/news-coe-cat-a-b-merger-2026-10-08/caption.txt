LTA wants to merge Cat A and Cat B COE into one category for all cars.

On top of that single premium, a "feebate" would kick in based on the car's value. Cheaper models get a rebate. Pricier models pay a surcharge. Mid-range pays the premium as is.

The reason: at the 7 October bidding exercise, Cat A closed at $130,001 and Cat B at $130,100. A $99 gap. The split between small and big cars has stopped sorting buyers by budget.

Two options on the table:
3 bands: $15,000 rebate, neutral, $15,000 surcharge
5 bands: $15,000 or $7,500 rebate, neutral, $7,500 or $15,000 surcharge

Still undecided: three or five bands, what happens to Cat E, and whether renewals get the feebate too.

This is still a proposal. Feedback closes 2 November 2026, 11.59pm at go.gov.sg/coe-pcd-feedback. LTA shares its findings in the first half of 2027.
