The most expensive monsoon-season repairs almost always start with something you could have checked in five minutes.

Six checks before the next downpour:
• Tyres: 3mm tread or more on every wheel
• Wipers: replace any blade that streaks
• Brakes: firm pedal, even bite
• Lights: every bulb working
• Demist: aircon and rear demister tested
• Washer fluid: topped up

A 10-minute check beats a 2-hour breakdown on the PIE.
