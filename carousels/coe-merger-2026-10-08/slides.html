How it works · 03/05
The "feebate"

Your car's value decides the final bill.

01
Everyone bids in one pool
Cat A and Cat B buyers share one quota and pay one COE premium.
02
LTA ranks car models by value
Each model's median open market value (OMV) is ranked and split into bands by percentile.
03
Your band adjusts the price
Lower-value model: rebate
Mid-range model: no change
Higher-value model: surcharge
04
Bands reviewed every year
A model can move to a different band as car prices shift.

The numbers · 04/05
Two options on the table

Up to $15,000 off, or $15,000 more.

Option 1 · Three bands
Simpler to run. Bigger jump between bands.

Option 2 · Five bands
More gradual steps between car values.

−$7,500Rebate
$0No change
+$7,500Surcharge
+$15,000Surcharge
← Lower car valueHigher car value →
Example: if the merged COE settled near $130,001, a $15,000 rebate cuts it by about 11.5%. A $15,000 surcharge takes it past $145,000.
