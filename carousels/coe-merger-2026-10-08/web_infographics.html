The proposal in one picture

Two COE queues become one.

Today a small car and a big car bid in separate queues. Under LTA's proposal, every car joins one queue and pays one COE price. Then the price is nudged up or down based on how expensive the car model is.

Still a proposal

Your car's price decides the band. Not its engine size.

LTA is exploring this method. It is not in use yet, and the final cut-offs are not set.
STEP 1

Find each model's typical price

LTA takes the median OMV of every car model, worked out from past registrations of that model.

STEP 2

Rank all models

Every model is lined up from the cheapest to the most expensive.

STEP 3

Split into bands

The line is cut into 3 or 5 bands by percentile. Bands are reviewed and published every year.
